Privacy Policy for Laser Gridlock

Last updated: 8 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Everylink ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with the Laser Gridlock mobile game (the "App"). We built Laser Gridlock to work without accounts, sign-in, or a server backend — your puzzle progress lives on your device. This policy covers the few places data does leave your device: advertising and in-app purchases.

Information We Collect

Information stored only on your device

Your level progress, star ratings, streak, hint/skip wallet, settings, and tutorial state are saved locally on your device and are never transmitted to us. We have no server and no user accounts, so we cannot see, access, or recover this data — uninstalling the App or clearing its data removes it permanently.

Advertising data (via Google AdMob)

The App shows banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google's SDK may collect:

  • Advertising identifiers (e.g. Android Advertising ID)
  • Device information (device type, operating system, general region)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, completions)

Where required (including in the EEA/UK), the App requests your consent for personalized advertising via Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) before any ad is requested. You can change your ad preferences at any time in your device's system settings (e.g. "Opt out of Ads Personalization").

See Google's own policy for details: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses data from AdMob partner sites/apps.

Purchases (via Google Play Billing / Apple In-App Purchase)

The App offers optional in-app purchases (removing ads, hint/skip packs, a cosmetic theme pack). Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store — we never see or store your payment details (card numbers, billing address, etc.). We only receive confirmation that a purchase succeeded, which we use to unlock the corresponding content locally on your device.

Anonymous gameplay analytics

The App records anonymous gameplay events (e.g. a level was completed, a hint was used, a tutorial was viewed) to help us understand which levels are too easy or too hard. These events contain gameplay facts only — no name, email, precise location, or device identifier is attached by us.

Children's Privacy

Laser Gridlock is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us at the address below and we will address it, including by directing ad requests to Google's designated-mixed-audience or child-directed ad settings where applicable.

Data Retention & Deletion

On-device data (progress, settings, purchases record) is deleted automatically when you uninstall the App or clear its data in your device settings. Advertising and purchase data held by Google/Apple is subject to their own retention policies and account controls.

Your Rights

Depending on your location (e.g. under GDPR or CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of certain data. Because most data about you is held by Google (AdMob, Play Billing) rather than us, the most direct way to exercise these rights for advertising data is through your Google Account's Ad Settings. For anything we hold, contact us at the address below.
